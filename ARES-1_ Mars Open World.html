🚀 ARES-1: Mars Open World

You landed on Mars. Explore, mine metal ⛏️ and ice ❄️, then build a base that keeps you alive. Check your air, food and power. Solar flares from NASA’s real archive will hit, so stay close to a shelter!

Move WASD or arrows · Look drag the mouse · Run Shift (uses more air) · Jump Space · Collect / read E · Build keys 1–4 or the buttons. On a phone: left thumb moves, right thumb looks.

  • Glowing pillars are discovery sites with real NASA facts (+3 metal).
  • Solar panels stop at night. MOXIE needs power to make air. Greenhouses need power to grow food. Nothing is free!

Data: DONKI flare events (archive), RAD/RadLab radiation, MOXIE, Mars Trek (Jezero), CSA food research, OSDR. Honest note: this page can’t reach NASA’s live servers, so data is curated and simplified, not live. Check the numbers before you cite them.
